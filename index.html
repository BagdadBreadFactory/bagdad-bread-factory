Our Heritage

Over Two Decades of Sweet Traditions in Chattogram

Established in 1998 in Oxygen, Bayezid, Bagdad Bread Factory & Sweets has transformed from a humble neighbourhood bakery into a trusted local institution. Our secret has never changed: combining time-honored baking methods with clean, high-quality ingredients.

Whether you need a daily loaf of fresh bread, fine festive sweets, or a custom milestone cake, our master bakers put absolute care into every creation.
